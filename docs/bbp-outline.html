Audience: parents, coaches, and new players on an American Under 12 sideline, and whoever maintains the card

TITLE
Enough to Survive the First Whistle
A pocket card for American Under 12 rugby: the USA tackle line, and the 10-a-side youth game versus full 15s.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
Youth rugby in the US runs on a mix of national law, state youth cards, and local festival rules. You're standing on the sideline at a U12 game, and you want to know what's legal before you yell.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
The 10-a-side youth game and full 15s play by different rules, and shouts like "Push!" or "Lift him!" are often wrong at this age. Open the card, pick 10s or 15s, read what's on the card, then check yourself with the quiz. A sideline that knows the tackle line and cheers for the game the kids are actually playing.
Transition 1Sideline Rugby is one phone-sized card. Three ideas explain it: the national rules that hold everywhere, the two games it compares, and how it fits a real sideline.
